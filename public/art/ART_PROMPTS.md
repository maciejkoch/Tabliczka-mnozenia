Created with the built-in OpenAI `image_gen` tool on 2026-10-14 for **Kosmiczna wyprawa**. No external artwork was used. The selected original PNG outputs were copied into this project; the spacecraft preserves the generated alpha channel.

```text

Primary request: wide landscape illustrated outer-space scene in a playful premium stylized 3D paper illustration style.

Constraints: one background illustration only; no text, no letters, no UI, no interfaces, no logos, no watermarks, no spaceship, no characters. Not a mockup or screenshot.
```
